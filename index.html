How it works

You see the result before you commit to anything.

  1. Send me your shop's name

    A WhatsApp message is enough. Add a few photos if you have them.

  2. Get a demo on your phone

    I build a first version with your name and details, and send you the link.

  3. Tell me what to change

    Prices, photos, colours, words. We adjust it until it feels right.

  4. Go live

    Your site goes online at its own web address, and I can keep it updated each month.
